# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Give and receive feedback in good faith.
- Focus discussion on the project and its community.
- Respect privacy and obtain permission before sharing another person's
  personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized language or
imagery, deliberate intimidation, and publication of private information
without permission are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This Code applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public.
Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communications that
violate it, and may issue warnings or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when appropriate.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to a repository maintainer through GitHub. Reports
will be handled promptly, fairly, and with respect for the reporter's privacy.
Do not file a public issue for a sensitive conduct report.

## Attribution
